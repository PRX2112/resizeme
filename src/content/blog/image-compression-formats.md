## Overview

Choosing the right compression format can dramatically reduce file size while preserving quality.

### WebP
- Supports both lossy and lossless.
- Better compression than JPEG for similar quality.

### AVIF
- Based on AV1 codec, offers even smaller sizes.
- Browser support is growing.

### JPEG
- Universal compatibility, but larger files.
- Best for photographs where AVIF/WebP aren't supported.

## How to Choose
1. Use **WebP** for most web images.
2. Fall back to **JPEG** for legacy browsers.
3. Opt for **AVIF** when you need maximum compression and can ensure browser support.
