## Why Image Performance Matters

Images often account for the majority of a page's bytes. Slow loading images can drastically affect Core Web Vitals and user experience. In this guide we cover:

- Choosing the right format (WebP, AVIF)
- Proper sizing and responsive `srcset`
- Lazy loading strategies
- CDN caching tips

By the end you’ll have a checklist to ensure every visual asset on your site loads fast.
